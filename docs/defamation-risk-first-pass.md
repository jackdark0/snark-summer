# Defamation-Risk First Pass - Ryle Kittenhouse Destiny Transcripts

This is a triage lead sheet, not a legal conclusion. It flags transcript moments
that could create defamation risk because they appear to make or repeat factual
claims about identifiable people or groups involving crime, sexual misconduct,
doxing, swatting, professional dishonesty, blackmail, extortion, or coordinated
harassment.

## Scope

- Source set: `transcripts/ryle-kittenhouse/raw/ryle-destiny-transcript-inventory-20260602.tsv`
- Processed transcripts reviewed: 211 Destiny-related Ryle Kittenhouse transcripts
- Method: agentic-style prefilter, then manual de-duplication into leads
- Clips: skipped for now; each lead includes a clip-ready YouTube timestamp
- Evidence status: auto-caption text only unless otherwise noted
- Actor classification: `Snark Server` is treated as a coordination hub; source
  titles and quotes preserve their original wording.
- Ally scope: retained leads only when the reputationally risky claim is aimed
  at a target/team-adjacent actor, currently Destiny/DGG, Dan Saltman, Foodshops,
  or another registered ally.

Legal orientation used only as a heuristic: U.S. defamation analysis often turns
on whether a statement can reasonably be read as asserting actual facts, and
public-figure cases can add an actual-malice layer. See `Milkovich v. Lorain
Journal Co.` (`https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/497/1/`) and
`New York Times Co. v. Sullivan`
(`https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/376/254/`). This report does not
apply state-specific law or privilege defenses.

## Risk Levels

- **High:** specific factual accusation of serious misconduct against an
  identifiable person.
- **Medium:** serious accusation, but framed through quotation, review,
  lawsuit-summary, chat/donation text, group reference, or conditional wording.
- **Low / watch:** reputationally negative, but more likely opinion, insult,
  hyperbole, or too vague without more context.
