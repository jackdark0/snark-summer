### EX-0021 | Permission Structures
**Source:** Chudlogic reaction stream (TechOne clip)  
**Date:** 2026-05-14  
**Timestamp:** 00:09:25  
**Clip URL:** PENDING  
**Status:** TIMESTAMP

**What happened:** In a video reacting to commentary creator Mtash, TechOne opened by noting Canadian defamation law differs from US anti-SLAPP law and that things critics say about him could "open them up to a lawsuit." He framed this as "wanting to help" while establishing that critics face legal exposure.
